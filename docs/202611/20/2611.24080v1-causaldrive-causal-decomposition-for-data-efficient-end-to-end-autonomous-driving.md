## 摘要
高效利用专家演示对端到端自动驾驶至关重要。然而，直接模仿专家轨迹会将名义意图（指定车辆应前往何处）与多模态驾驶风格（决定该意图如何执行）耦合在一起。这些因素的不同组合可能产生相似的专家轨迹，从而掩盖它们各自的因果贡献，并迫使策略从单一纠缠目标中同时学习二者。这种歧义限制了策略有效利用专家演示的能力。我们提出 CausalDrive，一种面向数据高效驾驶策略学习的因果分解框架，将确定性的名义意图预测与概率性的驾驶风格建模分离。具体而言，我们通过去除风格相关效应，从导航指令、地图拓扑和自车状态构建反事实名义轨迹，为名义意图规划器提供独立于专家轨迹的监督。以预测的名义意图和周围智能体为条件，基于扩散的风格执行策略将多样化驾驶风格建模为名义轨迹与专家轨迹之间残差的多模态分布。这种因果结构化监督减少了常规场景中对共享名义行为的冗余学习，将专家监督引导至信息丰富的执行变化，并提升数据利用率。CausalDrive 在 NAVSIM v1 上达到 91.2 PDMS，在 NAVSIM v2 上达到 89.5 EPDMS，达到或超过当前最先进性能。进一步实验表明，在有限专家数据下仍具有强劲性能，并在具有挑战性的交互场景中展现出明显优势。

## Abstract
Efficient use of expert demonstrations is essential for end-to-end autonomous driving. However, directly imitating expert trajectories couples nominal intention, which specifies where the vehicle should go, with multimodal driving styles, which determine how that intention is executed. Different combinations of these factors can yield similar expert trajectories, obscuring their respective causal contributions and forcing the policy to learn both from a single entangled target. This ambiguity limits how effectively the policy can exploit expert demonstrations. We propose CausalDrive, a causal decomposition framework for data-efficient driving policy learning that separates deterministic nominal intention prediction from probabilistic driving style modeling. Specifically, we construct counterfactual nominal trajectories from navigation command, map topology, and ego state by removing style dependent effects, providing a Nominal Intention Planner with supervision independent of expert trajectories. Conditioned on the predicted nominal intention and surrounding agents, a diffusion-based Style Execution Policy models diverse driving styles as a multimodal distribution over residuals between nominal and expert trajectories. This causally structured supervision reduces redundant learning of shared nominal behavior in routine scenarios, directing expert supervision toward informative execution variations and improving data utilization. CausalDrive achieves 91.2 PDMS on NAVSIM v1 and 89.5 EPDMS on NAVSIM v2, matching or surpassing state-of-the-art performance. Further experiments demonstrate strong performance with limited expert data and clear advantages in challenging interactive scenarios.
