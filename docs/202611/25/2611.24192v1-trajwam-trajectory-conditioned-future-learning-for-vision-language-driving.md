## 摘要
世界动作模型（WAMs）将未来场景预测与自动驾驶的动作规划相连接。每条驾驶记录将实际执行的自车轨迹与其观测到的未来配对，为学习这种连接提供了事实性结果。我们提出 TrajWAM，一种轨迹条件的世界动作模型，通过辅助鸟瞰图（BEV）未来学习提升视觉-语言驾驶。自车轨迹查询以共享规划器表示对与 DINOv3 对齐的 BEV 预测进行条件化。训练结合了在执行轨迹下的事实重建与在无标签扰动轨迹下的单侧特征分离。这些目标共同训练共享规划器生成驾驶路点。原生推理直接生成路点，并绕过辅助未来预测分支。在全部 220 条 Bench2Drive 路线中，TrajWAM 取得 91.53 的驾驶分数和 75.45% 的成功率，相较调度匹配的仅规划微调基线高出 5.31 个驾驶分数点和 8 条成功路线。

## Abstract
World Action Models (WAMs) connect future scene prediction with action planning for autonomous driving. Each driving record pairs an executed ego trajectory with its observed future, providing a factual outcome for learning this connection. We present TrajWAM, a trajectory-conditioned WAM that improves vision-language driving through auxiliary bird's-eye-view (BEV) future learning. An ego-trajectory query conditions DINOv3-aligned BEV predictions from shared planner representations. Training combines factual reconstruction under the executed trajectory with one-sided feature separation under an unlabeled perturbed trajectory. Together, these objectives train the shared planner to generate driving waypoints. Native inference directly generates waypoints and bypasses the auxiliary future-prediction branch. On all 220 Bench2Drive routes, TrajWAM achieves 91.53 Driving Score and 75.45% Success Rate, exceeding a schedule-matched planning-only fine-tuning baseline by 5.31 Driving Score points and eight successful routes.
