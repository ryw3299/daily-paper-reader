## 摘要
AutoE2E 是由 Autoware Foundation Robotaxi 工作组开发的开源端到端驾驶模型。它引入了一种时序鸟瞰图（BEV）架构，该架构结合多相机感知、高清地图信息和路线条件来预测未来车辆运动。所评估的模型使用预训练的相机-BEV 骨干网络、时序特征融合以及轻量级循环轨迹规划器。它包含约 7990 万个参数，其中在评估配置中约有 145 万个参数可训练。v1.0 版本提供了模型检查点、训练与评估代码，以及 DataModelConsole——一个用于可视化和检查模型实验的仪表板。在 KITScenes 验证基准上，微调后的检查点在三秒预测时域下实现了 0.745 m 的平均位移误差（ADE）和 2.020 m 的最终位移误差（FDE）。论文还报告了与慕尼黑工业大学 EDGAR 平台开展的初步实车研究，以及与卡尔斯鲁厄理工学院正在进行的合作。AutoE2E v1.0 是一个早期的开放研究版本。其定量评估为开环评估，该模型尚未展示经过验证的闭环自动驾驶能力。该版本旨在为自动驾驶社区提供一个可检查的基线，用于进一步研究、实验和协作开发。

## Abstract
AutoE2E is an open-source end-to-end driving model developed by the Autoware Foundation Robotaxi Working Group. It introduces a temporal bird's-eye-view (BEV) architecture that combines multi-camera perception, HD-map information and route conditioning to predict future vehicle motion. The evaluated model uses a pretrained camera-BEV backbone, temporal feature fusion and a lightweight recurrent trajectory planner. It contains approximately 79.9 million parameters, of which approximately 1.45 million are trainable in the evaluated configuration. The v1.0 release provides model checkpoints, training and evaluation code, and DataModelConsole, a dashboard for visualizing and inspecting model experiments. On the KITScenes validation benchmark, the fine-tuned checkpoint achieves an average displacement error (ADE) of 0.745 m and a final displacement error (FDE) of 2.020 m at a three-second prediction horizon. The paper also reports preliminary real-vehicle research conducted with the Technical University of Munich's EDGAR platform, alongside ongoing collaboration with the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. AutoE2E v1.0 is an early open research release. Its quantitative evaluation is open-loop, and the model has not yet demonstrated validated closed-loop autonomous driving. The release is intended to provide the autonomous-driving community with an inspectable baseline for further research, experimentation and collaborative development.
