## 摘要
驾驶模型需要选择使用哪些能力，以及何时重新考虑其决策。我们提出 AgentMoT，一种内置智能体推理与系统调度的 Transformer 混合模型。由智能体专家、世界专家和动作专家组合形成三条联合训练路径，分别用于直接动作、视觉-语言-动作以及世界-动作计算。路由器选择一条原生路径，而智能体专家提供推理并选择技能。执行反馈会触发唤醒请求，智能体自调度器学习是重新考虑任务决策还是保持不变。强制请求会直接调用智能体。相对能力原则通过将额外计算或干预的收益与其成本相关联来指导这些选择。在开环初始化之后，双环演化利用闭环经验训练路由器、智能体和自调度器，同时由失败驱动的更新来改进技能与执行反馈。AgentMoT 在 Bench2Drive v0.0.3 和 v0.0.4 上取得当前最优结果，驾驶分数分别为 94.48/92.33，成功率为 85.45/82.73%。在禁用技能时，学习到的路由在 v0.0.3 上相比最强固定路径将驾驶分数提高 1.79 分，成功率提高 4.09 个百分点。消融研究进一步表明，闭环智能体学习、双视图查询和事件触发唤醒均带来增益。

## Abstract
Driving models need to choose which capabilities to use and when to reconsider their decisions. We introduce AgentMoT, a Mixture-of-Transformers with built-in agent reasoning and system scheduling. Combinations of agent, world, and action experts form three jointly trained pathways for direct-action, vision-language-action, and world-action computation. The Router selects a native pathway, while the agent expert supplies reasoning and selects skills. Execution feedback raises wake-up requests, and the agent self-scheduler learns whether to reconsider the task decision or keep it. Mandatory requests invoke the agent directly. The relative-capability principle guides these choices by relating the benefit of additional computation or intervention to its cost. After open-loop initialization, dual-loop evolution trains the Router, agent, and self-scheduler from closed-loop experience while failure-driven updates refine skills and execution feedback. AgentMoT achieves state-of-the-art results on Bench2Drive v0.0.3 and v0.0.4, with 94.48/92.33 driving score and 85.45/82.73% success rate, respectively. With skills disabled, learned routing improves over the strongest fixed pathway by 1.79 driving-score points and 4.09 percentage points of success rate on v0.0.3. Ablation studies further show gains from closed-loop agent learning, two-view queries, and event-triggered wake-up.
