## 摘要
检索质量是检索增强生成（RAG）可靠性的关键因素，尤其是在结构化知识图谱上执行检索时。基于图的检索需要就候选边评分、遍历终止以及检索证据的充分性做出决策。我们提出 GRASP，一种查询自适应图检索框架，在检索过程中联合处理这些决策。交叉注意力评分器对单个查询词元与候选边之间的细粒度交互进行建模，为边选择提供查询特定信号。学习式停止门控以查询、遍历状态和跳深度为条件来决定是否终止遍历，使检索深度能够适应当前检索状态。充分性分类器进一步在生成前评估已组装的路径，并在预测证据不完整时触发重新检索。我们在三个具有不同图规模和答案长度的生物医学问答基准上评估 GRASP，将生物医学问答作为评估基于图的 RAG 检索的代表性场景。在所评估的检索和生成指标上，GRASP 始终优于最强的学习型基线。在三个基准上，无支持主张率降低了 8.9% 至 13.2%，其中在生成答案最长的基准上降幅最大。在候选密度较高时，检索增益更为明显；消融结果表明，这三个组件提供了互补的改进。

## Abstract
Retrieval quality is a key factor in the reliability of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), particularly when retrieval is performed over structured knowledge graphs. Graph-based retrieval requires decisions about candidate-edge scoring, traversal termination, and the sufficiency of the retrieved evidence. We propose GRASP, a query-adaptive graph retrieval framework that jointly addresses these decisions during the retrieval process. A cross-attention scorer models fine-grained interactions between individual query tokens and candidate edges, providing query-specific signals for edge selection. A learned stopping gate conditions traversal termination on the query, traversal state, and hop depth, allowing the retrieval depth to adapt to the current retrieval state. A sufficiency classifier further evaluates the assembled path before generation and triggers re-retrieval when the evidence is predicted to be incomplete. We evaluate GRASP on three biomedical question-answering benchmarks with different graph scales and answer lengths, using biomedical question answering as a representative setting for evaluating graph-based RAG retrieval. GRASP consistently improves over the strongest learned baseline across the evaluated retrieval and generation metrics. The unsupported-claim rate is reduced by 8.9% to 13.2% across the three benchmarks, with the largest reduction on the benchmark with the longest generated answers. The retrieval gains are more pronounced under higher candidate density, while ablation results indicate that the three components provide complementary improvements.
